# Pokémon Agents — badges campaign copy

Site: https://pokemonagents.dev — the one link to lead with everywhere below
Repo: https://github.com/techykamatis/pokemon-agents (source; also linked from the site)

The angle: the 8 gym badges, and that each one unlocks on real dev work (not play
time). Four of them release a legendary. The card art already carries the full
badge list and what earns each, so captions stay short.

Assets in this folder:

Posts
- `posts/badges-twitter-x-1600x900.png` — landscape card for X/Twitter (16:9)
- `posts/badges-instagram-1080x1080.png` — square card for the IG feed (1:1)
- `posts/badges-instagram-threads-portrait-1080x1350.png` — portrait card (4:5), IG + Threads

Stories
- `stories/badges-instagram-threads-story-1080x1920.png` — 9:16 story card (IG / Threads / FB),
  held inside the safe band (clear of the top handle zone and bottom caption/action rail)

Motion
- `motion/pokemon-agents-badges.gif` — ~10s loop recorded from the demo on dummy data: the town
  with a legendary on patrol, then the trainer card showing four badges earned (gold) and four
  still to go (grey), with the stones each badge paid into the bag. Lead with this; the gif
  outperforms the static card on every platform.
- `motion/pokemon-agents-demo-full.gif` — the full ~42s product demo loop (same synthetic world),
  if you want the whole thing instead of just the badge screen.

Keep the non-affiliation line in the caption — this is a public fan project. The gifs are a
screen capture of Nintendo's artwork, so they live here as campaign assets but are not committed
to the repo (same rule as the launch demo gif).

## The 8 badges (what each actually takes)

| Badge | Earned by | Releases |
|-------|-----------|----------|
| ZEPHYR | your first commit | — |
| HIVE | 10 green test runs | — |
| PLAIN | fixing a red test in the same session | Raikou |
| FOG | your first merge | Suicune |
| STORM | a 7-day streak | Entei |
| MINERAL | 100 commits | — |
| GLACIER | a clean session (25+ tool calls, no errors) | — |
| RISING | 100 sessions | Lugia |

Everything above is straight from the game's rule table — don't round or embellish it.
