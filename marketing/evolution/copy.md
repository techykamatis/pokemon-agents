## The four ways a Pokémon evolves

| Way | What triggers it |
|-----|------------------|
| Level | commits, merges, green test runs, sessions and tool calls all add XP |
| Stone | a stone earned from a gym badge, for the species that evolve by stone |
| Friendship | active days, sessions and commits — day and night for Espeon and Umbreon |
| Trade | a session handing work to a subagent, holding an item where one is needed |

Straight from `src/rules.mjs` (`findEvolution`) — every Gen 1 and 2 rule from PokéAPI, down to
Tyrogue branching on commits against green tests. Don't round or embellish it.
